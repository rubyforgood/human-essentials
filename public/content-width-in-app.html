  • Below 1600 nothing moves at all. A laptop sees exactly what it sees today — the 1440 pair above is identical in both states, which is the point of showing it.
  • The heading moves with the whole page, not away from it. At 2560 the h1 sits at 640 on the item form and at 640 on the items list, because the cap applies to every page. Nothing jumps as you navigate — which is what centring the card alone would have broken.
  • The wide tables stop being stretched. Distributions needs 1505px and gets 1534 instead of 2238.
  • The trade: at 2560 you get 352px of letterboxing on each side. In exchange the table row is 700px shorter and the form is no longer alone at one edge of the screen.
