Today works. It just has nowhere to go when you meet it.

Two of your three premises hold, one does not, and the app has already answered this question three times in other places — Today follows none of them.

So the answer to "what is the value in having it" is not a matter of taste — the app already has a rule, and this button is one of five controls not keeping it.

The options

A — Disable Today while today is already on screen recommended

August 2026 ← dimmed: you are already here

Live — press Next, and Today becomes available.

  • For: it is the app's own pagination rule, and the reason behind that rule applies here harder. Today flips in and out on every Prev and Next, so a Today that appeared and vanished would shift Prev and Next sideways constantly.
  • For: it answers your question in the interface rather than in a document — dimmed means "nothing to do here", so it stops reading as broken.
  • For: the button still tells you something when disabled: you are looking at today. That is information the List view currently has nowhere else.
  • Detail worth deciding: aria-disabled="true", not a real disabled. A genuinely disabled button leaves the tab order, so the toolbar's number of tab stops would change as you navigate — the same moving-target defect as changing width, one level up. design.md's "form actions get a real disabled" exists so a form cannot submit; Today submits nothing.

B — Hide Today until it means something

August 2026

Live — press Next and watch Prev and Next jump sideways.

  • For: also an app precedent — it is exactly what "Clear all" does.
  • Against: "Clear all" appears when you filter, which is rare and deliberate. Today would appear and disappear on every single Prev and Next, moving its neighbours each time. Try the demo above.

C — Remove Today altogether

  • For: the month and year selects can now get you back, so it is no longer the only route home. One less control.
  • Against: two interactions instead of one, and you have to know today's date to use them — which is precisely what someone pressing Today does not want to think about.
  • Against: Google Calendar, Outlook, Apple Calendar and Notion all have it. It is close to a universal affordance, and the cost of keeping it is one dimmed button.
  • Against: in the List view it is currently the only thing that locates today at all.

D — Leave it as it is

Defensible — Google keeps Today always enabled too. But it is the thing you have now reported twice, and "it is fine, the convention is odd" is a worse answer than a dimmed button.

Recommendation

A, with aria-disabled, plus two fixes that come with it.

  1. Today is dimmed while today is on screen and live once you navigate away. The app's pagination rule, applied to the control it was written for.
  2. Mark today in the List view. A real defect, independent of this: on a phone the list is the default and nothing on it says which day is now.
  3. The three "Reset search" buttons get the same treatment — otherwise this is one fix to one button and the same inconsistency stays in three other places.

I would not remove Today. Once it is honest about its state it is doing real work: when it is live it takes you home in one click, and when it is dimmed it is telling you that you are already there.
