A “showing” line under the filter bar

Nothing here is built. Three placements for the sentence, and the thing you need to decide first: date_range_label cannot currently produce it correctly — four of its twelve outputs are wrong, ambiguous or empty.

1What it is for

date_range_label does not return a label. It returns a sentence fragment — an adverbial phrase built to be glued onto a noun phrase:

Showing 13 distributions over the last 30 days.

That is why it says “over the last 30 days” and “during the period…” rather than “Last 30 days”. It was written to complete a sentence and has never had a sentence to complete. Four jobs such a line does:

  • Confirms the filter landed. Pressing Filter reloads the page; prose confirms what you are now looking at.
  • Names the window the totals describe. The figures below say “Distributions 13”. They do not say 13 out of what period.
  • Survives scroll and print. The control is pinned at the top. A <select> prints as a box; a sentence prints as a sentence.
  • Rescues the empty state. “No distributions over the last 30 days” answers “did I filter wrong, or is there genuinely nothing?” — which “No distributions match those filters” does not.

2Why it cannot ship as it stands

Every output, measured just now against the real presets:

PresetProducesVerdict
Today“today”fine
Yesterday“yesterday”fine
Last 7 days“over the last week”disagrees with the option, which says 7 days
Last 30 days“over the last 30 days”fine
This month“this month”fine
Last month“last month”fine
Last 12 months“last 12 months”fine
Prior year“prior year”fine
This year“during the period 01 Jan to 31 Dec”no year. Falls to the generic branch instead of saying “this year”
All time“during the period 19 Aug to 19 Aug”wrong. Reads as one day. The range is 1926–2027
Default“during the period 19 Jun to 19 Sep”no year
Custom, ending today“since 2026-03-03”raw ISO, where every other line uses “19 Jun”
Custom, starting today“” (empty)empty. The sentence becomes “Showing 13 distributions.”

One root cause behind most of it. selected_range_described formats with to_fs(:short), and Rails' :short for a Date is "%d %b" — day and month, no year. That is why a century-wide range collapses to “19 Aug to 19 Aug”.

The rest is that This year and All time have no when clause of their own, so they fall through to the date-range wording instead of naming themselves.

3Where the line goes

All three use the same corrected sentence. The filter bar below is the app's real markup, not a drawing.

4What I would do

Fix date_range_label first — it is four bugs, not a refactor: give This year and All time their own clauses, format with the year, and make the start-date-is-today case say something rather than nothing. DateRangeHelper has no test for this method at all today.

Then B and C, not A: the caption puts the period where the totals are, and the empty state is where a user is actually asking the question the sentence answers. A is the one placement that duplicates something already on screen.

If you want A anyway, it should replace the count in the band rather than sit above it — but that is a bigger change to a component eleven pages share.

So: A, B, C, or B+C? And shall I fix the helper as part of it? I would do B+C with the helper fixed and specs added.
