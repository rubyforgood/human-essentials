Can a clipped cell show its full text on hover?

Yes — and unlike the last three times a tooltip came up, here it is the standard answer. Hover the long comments below, and Tab into the last table to see the difference focus makes.

Why this one is different

I have argued against a tooltip three times in this work — on the scan button, the remove button and the clipped cell — and those were the same case each time: a control that needed a name, where a word solved it for free and a tooltip was a component bought to avoid typing one.

Truncated text is the opposite case. The words are already written, already in the DOM, and deliberately hidden to keep the table scannable. Revealing them on demand is the textbook use of a tooltip, and the design systems say so: Carbon ships a documented "tooltip on truncated table text" pattern, Ant Design has ellipsis: {showTitle: true} on table columns and recommends a real Tooltip over it, AG Grid has tooltipField built in, and Salesforce pairs slds-truncate with a title. The pattern is clip plus reveal, not clip alone.

Recommendation

Option C — a real tooltip on hover and focus, and only on cells whose text is actually clipped.

Only-when-clipped is the part that makes it affordable. A tooltip repeating text you can already read is noise, and making every cell focusable would add a tab stop per row — 42 of them on /adjustments. Comparing scrollWidth with clientWidth per cell means only the cells that are hiding something become interactive. In the table above that is ? of four.

WCAG 1.4.13 is the whole reason this needs a controller rather than a title. Content shown on hover or focus must be dismissible (Escape closes it without moving the pointer), hoverable (you can move onto the tooltip to read or select the text) and persistent (it does not time out). title is none of those, shows nothing on keyboard focus, does not exist on touch, and is announced on top of the cell text a screen reader already read.

Touch is the honest gap: there is no hover on a phone, and a tap target the size of a table cell fights the row. The detail page stays the answer there, which is what it already is.
