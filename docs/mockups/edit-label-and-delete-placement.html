1. Is "Make a correction" right? — Nomeasured

LabelCall sitesWhere
Edit30Vendors, Storage locations, Items, Donation sites, every admin page, the partner portal
Make a correction4Donations, Purchases, Distributions, Product drives

It is inherited, not chosen. The decision log records it as pre-existing wording kept during the migration — "the pre-migration purchase page carried exactly two actions, Make a correction and a delete button, and both are still there." No entry argues for it.

And design.md's own rule says a button label is a verb and its object, two or three words. "Make a correction" is three, so it passes on length and fails on meaning: it assumes the record is wrong. You might be adding a tag, fixing a date, or attaching a receipt. "Correction" narrows an ordinary action to one of its reasons.

Use "Edit". Thirty places in this app already do, and it is what GOV.UK, Polaris and Carbon all use for the same action. The one thing worth keeping is the sentence in the callout when a record is locked — "This purchase cannot be corrected or deleted" — because there "corrected" is prose explaining a restriction, not a control.

2. The placement — and it is not just the ordermeasured

design.md: "At most three actions, exactly one of them primary, primary last." This header has no primary, so Delete inherits the last slot by default — the most destructive thing on the page sits where the main action belongs. That is the reversal you are seeing.

It is also the only page in the app shaped this way. Donations, purchases and distributions put their Edit and Delete in a row at the foot of the page, and their headers carry Print and Start a distribution instead.

The wider question this exposes

Four record pages carry an Edit and a Delete, and they do not agree on where those go:

PageEdit + Delete live…Header carries
Product drivesin the page headerExport, Edit, Delete
Donationsat the foot of the pagePrint, Start a distribution
Purchasesat the foot of the pagePrint, Start a distribution
Distributionsat the foot of the pagePrint, Distribution complete

design.md is clear which is right: an action on the record as a whole belongs in the page header, and it names Deactivate as the example. So product drives has the placement right and the other three have it wrong — the opposite of what "this one looks odd" would suggest. Those three also have a genuine primary already, so their headers would go to four actions, which is one past the limit; the overflow menu in Option A is what makes them fit.

I have scoped this preview to the page you asked about. Say the word and the same treatment goes on the other three, which is where the inconsistency actually lives.

Recommendation

"Edit", and Option A. The label because thirty places already say it and the current one asserts something that may not be true. The placement because the last slot is the primary's, a delete should never inherit it by default, and this app already has the component that solves it.
