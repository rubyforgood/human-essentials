The filter bar, and how much room it should take

Two of the three things you spotted are defects I introduced last round. The third is a real design question. Nothing here is built.

1Two defects

The date range is two columns wide. I gave it sm:col-span-2 because it holds three inputs once Custom is chosen. But it only holds three inputs when Custom is chosen, and the rest of the time it is one select sitting in a cell twice the width of its neighbours. The span should follow the state, not be permanent.

The actions are on their own row, always. Measured on every page with a bar — even /partners, which has a single filter and could fit the whole thing on one line:

2Should the bar collapse?

Even fixed, /donations is three rows of controls above the data. The standard answer for a set this size is a disclosure: Jira, Linear, Notion and the AWS console all put a dense filter set behind a Filters button. GitHub goes the other way and keeps one search box; Stripe shows a row of chips with an "add filter" affordance.

The rule they all follow, and the one that matters: a collapsed filter set must still say what is active. Filters that narrow the data with nothing on screen to show for it are how people conclude their records have vanished.

3What I would build

The two fixes regardless — the conditional span and the inline actions. They are defects, not preferences, and they take a row off every page.

Then C, applied by size. A bar with four filters or fewer stays open: after the fix it is one row, and hiding one row behind a click is worse than showing it. A bar with five or more collapses, with chips for whatever is active. That is /donations (9), /distributions (7), /requests (6) and /events (5); the other twelve stay as they are.

The threshold lives in the partial, so no call site decides it and the behaviour cannot drift page to page.
