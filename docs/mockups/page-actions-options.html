How many buttons may a header carry?

One primary, at most three visible, primary last. Polaris, Carbon, Material and Atlassian all land in the same place: exactly one primary action; everything else secondary or ghost; beyond three, the least-used collapse behind “More actions”.

Measured here: six index pages already carry exactly three — partner agencies, donation sites, product drive participants, requests, storage locations, vendors — always two secondary and one primary. Eleven more carry two. So the app is already consistent and already at the ceiling; the rule just is not written down, which is why a fourth button had nowhere to go but into the table.

design.md’s page header section documents spacing and the back link and says nothing about how many actions or their weight. That is the gap.

To decide

A or B for the tab action. A is the industry pattern and needs tabs to become real URLs; B is a contained fix to how it looks. I would take A, and it is the larger change.
