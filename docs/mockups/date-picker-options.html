Date range picker — options, not built

Nothing here is implemented. The current picker is Litepicker, loaded from a CDN. It is not broken; it is foreign — every visual property differs from the design system, which is what makes it look odd next to everything else.

Three more things worth knowing.

It is pinned to https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/litepicker/dist/litepicker.js — unversioned. That is an external runtime dependency whose behaviour can change under the app without a deploy. The migration removed the Font Awesome CDNs deliberately; this one survived.

The preset labels are Title Case — "All Time", "Last 7 Days", "This Month" — against the house sentence case.

Invalid input raises a browser alert(). Every other validation error in the app renders in the page.

2How the industry does this

Three patterns, and which is right depends entirely on whether the user is choosing dates or naming a period.

3The complication, and a question for you

The server takes one string: filters[date_range] = "June 19, 2026 - September 19, 2026", split on " - " and parsed with Date.strptime(d, "%B %d, %Y"). A second hidden field, filters[date_range_label], carries the preset name so the page can say "over the last 30 days".

Option B naturally wants start_date and end_date. Two ways to land it:

Keep the wire format. The new control writes the combined string into the existing hidden field. Nothing server-side changes, ten call sites keep working, and the change is contained to one partial plus a Stimulus controller. What I would do first.

Or change the parsing to accept two ISO dates. Cleaner — strptime with a localised month name is fragile, and its rescue silently resets to the default range with a flash. But it touches the helper, ten call sites and their specs.

So: A, B or C — and keep the wire format or change it? I would take B, keeping the wire format, and treat the parsing as a separate follow-up.
