5. The axis label

Measured: the last label is 29px tall against every other one at 14, because “so far” sits under it on a second line. That is the asymmetry — one column's label is twice the height of its neighbours.

Now

The axis is 12px deeper than it needs to be, for one word under one label.

Option A — take it off the axis recommended

The month still reads as unfinished from its lighter fill and outline, and the words are still there twice: the card subtitle says “Sep 2026 is still running” and the table header for that column says “so far”.

Every label the same height. Two words and a fill, none of them the axis.

Option B — one line, with a mark

An asterisk keeps a signal on the chart at one line height, with a note under it: * September is still running.

Symmetric, and the chart still says something. The asterisk is one more convention to learn, and it is silent to anyone not reading the note.
Recommendation: chips onto the chart as its key, and “so far” off the axis.

The two field problems are one problem — chips inside a field make the field grow — and moving them below the chart solves both at once, because a key that grows downwards cannot push the thing it explains. It also replaces Highcharts' own legend rather than sitting beside it.

For the axis, the marking is already carried three other ways: the lighter fill, the table header, and the card subtitle. The axis is the one place it costs symmetry, so it is the one to drop. If you would rather the chart itself still said something, B keeps an asterisk at one line height.
