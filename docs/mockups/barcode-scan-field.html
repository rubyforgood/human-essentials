You are right on both counts: the design system already settles this, and what I added does not match it. Measured on the running app.

1. What I put on the page wrong, and it spills

An icon floated inside the field with absolute right-2 top-8. The field is 38px tall, the button 36px, and the absolute offset is tuned to one particular label height — so on this form it sits 4px below the field's bottom edge and is not vertically centred. That is the spill you saw.

4px low →

I did not invent this bug — I copied it. The two barcode modals have the same markup and the same 4px spill, measured: input 38px, button 36px, not centred.

2. What audits already does the design system's answer

The scan bar on the inventory audit — and on donations, purchases and distributions, since they share line_items/_line_item_table — joins the field and the button into one rounded rectangle sharing a border. Measured there: both exactly 38px, both 700→738, perfectly centred, no spill possible.

And design.md already says so, in the line items section: "The scan field and its button are joined — one rounded rectangle sharing a border — rather than two controls with a gap between them. That is what keeps them the same height by construction; as separate boxes they had drifted to 38px and 42px."

3. Recommendation

Use the joined pattern, and use it in all three places — the new barcode form I just changed, and the two barcode modals that have carried the same 4px spill all along. One pattern, one rendering, and the height cannot drift because the two controls share a border rather than being positioned against each other.
